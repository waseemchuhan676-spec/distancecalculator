Route Overview: Toronto to Ottawa

Connecting Canada's largest metropolis with the nation's capital, the 450-kilometer (280-mile) drive traverses the scenic north shore of Lake Ontario before turning north through eastern Ontario farmland along the Rideau Canal corridor.

The Standard Highway Route

From downtown Toronto, travel east on the Macdonald-Cartier Freeway (Highway 401) through Oshawa, Cobourg, Belleville, and Kingston. Near Prescott (Interchange 721), merge onto Highway 416 North (Veterans Memorial Highway), which leads directly into central Ottawa.

Highway 7 Scenic Route (Alternative)

Drivers looking to avoid the heavy commercial truck traffic of Highway 401 can take Highway 7 via Peterborough and Perth. While this route is slightly shorter geographically (~400 km), lower rural speed limits and traffic lights through small towns make the total travel time around 5 to 5.5 hours.

❄️ Winter Driving Note: Highway 401 between Cobourg and Kingston is prone to sudden lake-effect snow squalls coming off Lake Ontario from December through March. Check Ontario 511 road reports before departing.

Top Recommended Stops En Route

  1. The Big Apple (Colborne): Famous highway bakery landmark featuring apple pies and fresh local pastries right off the 401.
  2. Kingston, Ontario: Located at the midpoint (~260 km from Toronto), historic Kingston offers waterfront dining, Fort Henry, and Queen's University.
  3. 1000 Islands Parkway: A short scenic detour along the St. Lawrence River between Gananoque and Brockville.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Toronto to Ottawa by car?

The road distance is approximately 450 kilometers (280 miles) via Highways 401 and 416.

Is VIA Rail train service available between Toronto and Ottawa?

Yes, VIA Rail operates frequent daily corridor train services between Toronto Union Station and Ottawa Train Station, taking approximately 4 hours and 20 minutes.
