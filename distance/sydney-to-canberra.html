Route Overview: Sydney to Canberra

The journey between Sydney and Canberra is one of Australia's premier dual-carriageway intercity drives, spanning 280 kilometers (174 miles) through the rolling Southern Highlands and Tablelands of New South Wales into the Australian Capital Territory.

The Highway Motorway Corridor (M31 to M23)

Departing southern Sydney, drivers take the M5 South-Western Motorway transitioning directly into the Hume Motorway (M31). The motorway climbs steadily through the Southern Highlands past Mittagong, Berrima, and Marulan. Just south of Goulburn, take the exit onto the Federal Highway (M23), which skirts the shores of Lake George before entering North Canberra via the Majura Parkway or Federal Highway.

🦘 Wildlife & Safety Caution: Eastern Grey Kangaroos frequently cross the Federal Highway around Lake George and the ACT border at dawn and dusk. Drive with extra vigilance during twilight hours.

Top Recommended Stops Along the Route

  1. Bowral & Southern Highlands: Just off the Hume Motorway (~115 km from Sydney), famous for cool-climate vineyards, antique shops, and the Bradman Cricket Museum.
  2. Goulburn: Australia's first inland city, located roughly midway. Home to the towering 15-meter "Big Merino" landmark, bakeries, and extensive fuel stations.
  3. Lake George Lookout: A scenic viewing pull-over overlooking an endorheic lake that mysteriously fills and completely vanishes over multi-year weather cycles.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Sydney to Canberra by car?

The road distance is approximately 280 kilometers (174 miles) along the Hume and Federal Highways.

What is the highway speed limit on this drive?

Most of the Hume Motorway and Federal Highway is signed at 110 km/h (68 mph) once outside metropolitan Sydney.
