Route Overview: LA to San Diego

Connecting the two largest cities in California, the drive between Los Angeles and San Diego spans 120 miles (193 km) along the Pacific coastline. It is one of the most popular weekend getaway routes in the western United States, traversing Orange County and northern San Diego County.

Primary Highway Routes

1. Interstate 5 South (The Direct Highway)

I-5 is the fastest and most direct corridor. From downtown Los Angeles, head south through Anaheim, Irvine, and San Juan Capistrano. You then drive alongside the ocean through the Marine Corps Base Camp Pendleton before entering North San Diego County (Oceanside, Carlsbad, Encinitas) and arriving in downtown San Diego. Clear traffic duration is approximately 2 hours.

2. Pacific Coast Highway / Highway 1 (The Scenic Option)

For travelers who prioritize ocean vistas over speed, take Highway 1 through Huntington Beach, Newport Beach, and Laguna Beach before reconnecting with I-5 at Dana Point. Expect this scenic detour to add 1 to 1.5 hours to your driving time.

⚠️ Traffic Warning: Heavy congestion regularly occurs around Irvine/El Toro 'Y' interchange in Orange County and through the Camp Pendleton stretch on Sunday afternoons as weekend travelers head north.

Tolls & Transit Options

Standard Interstate 5 has zero toll booths. However, drivers can opt to take the CA-73 Toll Road in southern Orange County to bypass traffic at the I-405/I-5 junction. Tolls are collected electronically through FasTrak or online pay-by-plate.

Top Recommended Stops En Route

  1. San Juan Capistrano: Historic Spanish colonial mission founded in 1776, located midway along the drive.
  2. San Clemente Pier: Beautiful Spanish village by the sea with classic coastal boardwalk dining.
  3. Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve: Dramatic maritime cliffs and scenic hiking trails overlooking the Pacific near La Jolla.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Los Angeles from San Diego by car?

The road distance is approximately 120 miles (193 km) from city center to city center via Interstate 5.

Can I take the train instead of driving?

Yes! Amtrak's Pacific Surfliner operates several daily trains between LA Union Station and San Diego Santa Fe Depot, taking approximately 2 hours and 50 minutes with stunning coastline views right along the surf.
