How Palworld breeding works

Every Pal species carries a hidden number called breeding power (CombiRank). In Palworld 1.0 it runs from 10 for the rarest Pals to 3100 for the most common — lower means rarer. Anubis is 480; Lamball is 3050.

For an ordinary pair, the game averages both parents' breeding power, rounds up, and picks the Pal in the 183-entry formula pool whose breeding power is closest to that average:

Target = (Parent A + Parent B) ÷ 2 (all 1.0 breeding powers are even numbers, so this is exact). The offspring is the pool Pal nearest the target.

Special combinations override the formula: 164 fixed parent pairs always produce one specific child. For example, Relaxaurus + Sparkit always gives Relaxaurus Lux, whatever the formula would say.

Same-species pairs always breed true: two Pals of the same species produce that species. Some Pals, such as Grizzbolt, Faleris and Shadowbeak, can only be bred this way in Palworld 1.0.

One gender-dependent pair exists: Katress + Wixen. A female Katress with a male Wixen gives Katress Ignis; a male Katress with a female Wixen gives Wixen Noct.

What you need to breed

  • Breeding Farm — unlocked at Technology Level 19; costs 100 Wood, 20 Stone and 50 Fiber.
  • One male and one female Pal assigned to the farm.
  • 1 Cake per egg in the farm's chest. A Cake is crafted from 5 Flour, 8 Red Berries, 7 Milk, 8 Eggs and 2 Honey.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Palworld breeding calculator work?

Pick two parent Pals and the calculator shows the exact offspring. It checks the 164 fixed special combinations first, then the same-species rule, and otherwise averages both parents' hidden breeding power (CombiRank), rounds up, and returns the closest Pal in the 183-entry formula pool. Reverse lookup lists every parent pair that produces the Pal you pick.

What is CombiRank (breeding power)?

CombiRank is a hidden number every Pal species carries in Palworld 1.0, from 10 for the rarest Pals to 3100 for the most common. Lower means rarer: for example, Anubis is 480 and Lamball is 3050. It is not a combat stat — it only decides breeding results.

Can I breed any two Pals together?

Any two Pals that can enter a Breeding Farm can breed together, as long as one is male and one is female. Two Pals that cannot use the Breeding Farm — Panthalus and Astralym — are not listed as parents. You also need 1 Cake in the farm's chest for each egg.

Why does Penking + Bushi give Sibelyx instead of Anubis?

Palworld 1.0, released on 10 July 2026, completely rebuilt the breeding table. Penking + Bushi made Anubis in the old Early Access data, but in 1.0 the same pair gives Sibelyx. Older breeding guides and calculators that still use pre-1.0 data will give the wrong answer — this calculator uses verified 1.0 data only.

What do I need to breed Pals in Palworld?

Unlock the Breeding Farm at Technology Level 19 (100 Wood, 20 Stone, 50 Fiber), place one male and one female Pal in it, and keep at least 1 Cake in the farm's chest. One Cake is used per egg. A Cake is crafted from 5 Flour, 8 Red Berries, 7 Milk, 8 Eggs, and 2 Honey.

Why does Katress + Wixen give two different children?

Katress + Wixen is the one gender-dependent combination in Palworld 1.0. A female Katress with a male Wixen gives Katress Ignis, while a male Katress with a female Wixen gives Wixen Noct. The calculator shows both results and which genders produce each.
