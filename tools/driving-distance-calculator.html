Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate driving distance between cities?

Enter your starting city and destination city, then open the result in the main calculator with Car / Driving mode preselected. Driving distance follows road routing geometry, and the estimated car travel time uses a typical car cruising speed of 80 km/h.

How long does it take to drive between cities?

It depends on the driving distance between the two cities. As a planning guide, divide the road distance by a typical car cruising speed of 80 km/h — for example, a 400 km drive takes about 5 hours before stops. Actual drive times vary with traffic, road conditions, and stops, so treat it as an estimate, not an exact arrival time.

How does the car travel time calculator work?

The car travel time calculator takes the road driving distance between your two cities and estimates travel time at a typical car cruising speed of 80 km/h. It is a planning estimate: traffic, road works, weather, and rest stops can make the real journey longer.

Is driving distance the same as straight-line distance?

No. Driving distance follows the actual road route between two cities, which is almost always longer than the straight-line distance. Use Car / Driving mode for road journeys and the straight-line figure only for comparison.

Can I calculate driving distance between cities in different countries?

Yes. The calculator supports global locations including cities and towns worldwide, so you can estimate driving distance for international city pairs as well.

Does this show the shortest driving distance between two points?

It shows the road driving distance between the two places you enter, following the road route a car would typically follow — not the straight-line distance. Live navigation apps may suggest a different route with current traffic, so treat this as a planning figure.

How do I find the driving distance between two places in miles by car?

Enter your two places, open the result in the main calculator with Car / Driving mode, and switch the unit toggle to Miles — the driving distance between the two places and the estimated driving time between cities are then shown in miles.
