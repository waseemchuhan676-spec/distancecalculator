Conversion Formulas & Mathematical Foundations

Converting fuel economy requires accounting for whether the metric measures efficiency (distance traveled per fuel unit) or consumption (fuel volume required per distance unit):

MPG (US) = 235.214583 ÷ (L/100 km)
MPG (UK) = 282.480936 ÷ (L/100 km)
km/L = 100 ÷ (L/100 km)
MPG (UK) = MPG (US) × 1.20095
MPG (US) = km/L × 2.35214583

Why is MPG UK Higher than MPG US?

An Imperial (UK) gallon is approximately 20% larger than a US customary liquid gallon:

  • 1 US Liquid Gallon: 3.785411784 liters (231 cubic inches)
  • 1 Imperial UK Gallon: 4.54609 liters (approx. 277.42 cubic inches)

Because an Imperial gallon contains more fuel, a vehicle will travel further on one Imperial gallon than on one US gallon, resulting in a numerical MPG value that is 20.095% higher for the exact same vehicle performance.

Efficiency vs. Consumption

In MPG and km/L, a higher number means superior efficiency (traveling further on less fuel). In L/100 km, a lower number means superior efficiency (burning fewer liters to travel 100 km).
