How to Convert KM to Miles in Your Head

If you don't have a calculator handy while driving in countries using miles (such as the United States or United Kingdom), multiply the kilometers by 0.6 for a quick estimate. For example, 100 km × 0.6 = 60 miles (exact is 62.14 miles).
