How to meet halfway on a road trip

Two people driving towards each other should meet at the point where both drives are equal — not at the straight-line middle of the map, which can sit far from any road. This tool walks the actual road route and stops exactly halfway, then names the nearest town so you have a real place to meet: a fuel station, restaurant, or city stop.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a halfway point between two cities?

The halfway point is the place along the road route where the driving distance from each city is equal. It is the fairest meeting point for two people travelling towards each other.

How is the halfway point calculated?

Both places are geocoded, the road route is fetched from OSRM (OpenStreetMap routing), and the point at exactly half of the total road distance along that route is taken as the halfway point. The nearest named town is then found by reverse geocoding.

Is the halfway point the same as the geographic midpoint?

Not always. The geographic midpoint is the straight-line middle between two coordinates, while this tool uses the midpoint along the actual road route, which is what matters for a road trip meet-up. If live road routing is unavailable, the tool falls back to the straight-line midpoint and says so.
