What is a cities-within-radius search?

A radius search draws a circle around a place and lists every city and town inside it. Travellers use it to plan day trips and stopovers, movers use it to compare nearby towns, and delivery planners use it to find coverage areas — without guessing which places fall inside the circle.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find cities within 50 km of a place?

Enter the place name, choose a radius such as 50 km, and the tool lists every mapped city, town, and village inside that circle, sorted by straight-line distance, with a map.

Where does the cities data come from?

Results come from OpenStreetMap via the Overpass API: places tagged as city, town, or village within the radius. Distances are straight-line (haversine) distances from the centre point.

Why is a city missing from the results?

A place appears only if it is mapped in OpenStreetMap with a city, town, or village tag inside the radius. Very small settlements may be unmapped, and results are capped at 100 places for large radii — try a smaller radius for complete local coverage.
