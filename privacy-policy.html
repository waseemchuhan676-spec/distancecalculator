Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At World Distance Calculator (accessible from https://distancecalculator-theta.vercel.app/), user privacy is of paramount importance to us. This document outlines the types of information we collect and how we utilize it.

1. Personal Data Collection

We do not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical street address.

2. Geolocation Permissions

If you choose to use the "My Location" feature, your browser requests permission to access your device's geographical coordinates. This coordinate data is processed solely within your browser session to resolve your starting point. It is never logged on our servers or linked to your identity.

3. Local Storage

We utilize your browser's standard LocalStorage mechanism solely to save your recent route calculation history on your own device for your convenience. This data remains on your client device and is never uploaded to our servers.

4. Analytics & Cookies

We use Google Analytics (gtag.js) to understand general traffic trends, such as page views and travel mode selections. Google Analytics uses cookies to gather anonymous diagnostic and aggregate usage data. No personal data or precise location is submitted to analytics.

5. External Services

When you query locations or request road routes, your queries are processed via public OpenStreetMap / Nominatim and OSRM endpoints. These requests are subject to the respective privacy and usage policies of the OpenStreetMap Foundation.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our GitHub repository: github.com/waseemchuhan676-spec/distancecalculator.
