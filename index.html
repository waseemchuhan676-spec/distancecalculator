How to Calculate Distance Between Two Cities

Whether you are planning a weekend road trip, calculating corporate travel mileage, or estimating flight duration, the World Distance Calculator provides precise measurements in both kilometers (km) and miles (mi).

1. Driving Distance vs Straight-Line Flight Distance

Straight-line distance (great-circle distance) represents the shortest direct path between two geographic coordinates across Earth's surface. In contrast, driving distance follows physical roads, highways, and bridges, meaning the driving route is typically 15% to 35% longer than air distance.

2. Travel Time Calculation

Travel times are calculated using typical cruising speeds for each vehicle category. For example, highway driving by car averages 80 km/h, while intercity commercial flights average 800 km/h.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate distance between two places?

Enter your starting location and destination, choose your travel mode (Car, Motorbike, Bus, Train, Flight, or Bicycle), and click 'Calculate Distance' to view total road distance, flight distance, estimated travel time, and optional fuel cost.

What is the difference between route distance and straight-line distance?

Straight-line distance (as the crow flies) is the shortest geographical path between two coordinates. Route distance is the actual driving or road distance following highways and roads, which is typically 15% to 35% longer.

Can I calculate distance between cities in different countries?

Yes, the World Distance Calculator supports global locations including cities, towns, airports, and geographical landmarks worldwide.

How is flight distance calculated?

Flight distance is calculated using the Great-Circle Haversine formula based on Earth's spherical curvature, representing the direct aerial flight corridor between two coordinates.

Can I calculate driving or road distance by car?

Yes! Select the Car / Driving mode to get real-time driving route distance along highways and streets powered by open road routing algorithms.

Can I estimate fuel cost for a road trip?

Yes. Check the 'Include fuel / trip cost' box, input your vehicle mileage (km/L) and local fuel price per liter, then select your preferred currency (PKR, USD, EUR, etc.) to get an instant cost estimate.

Can I switch between kilometers and miles?

Yes. Toggle between the KM and Miles buttons in the results panel to view all distance measurements in your preferred unit.

Are travel times exact?

Travel times are based on typical speed averages (e.g. 80 km/h for driving, 800 km/h for flights). Actual duration varies based on traffic, weather, road conditions, and stops.
