Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate distance between two places?

Enter a starting location and destination, choose a travel mode, then select Calculate Distance to see distance, straight-line distance and estimated travel time.

What is the difference between route distance and straight-line distance?

Straight-line distance is the shortest geographical path. Route distance follows an available travel corridor or estimate.

Can I calculate distance between cities in different countries?

Yes. Search for cities, countries, airports and other places worldwide.

How is flight distance calculated?

Flight distance is based on a great-circle estimate between geographical coordinates. Actual flight paths can vary.

Can I calculate driving or road distance?

The calculator attempts to retrieve public road routing data for supported non-flight journeys and uses an estimate if routing is unavailable.

Can I estimate fuel cost for a trip?

Yes. Enable fuel cost, enter efficiency, fuel price and currency to estimate fuel needed and total cost.

Can I switch between kilometers and miles?

Yes. Use the KM and Miles buttons in the results area.

Are travel times exact?

No. Travel times are estimates and can change because of traffic, routes, weather and schedules.
