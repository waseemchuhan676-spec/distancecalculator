Frequently Asked Questions

How is the distance calculated?

The tool uses geographical coordinates. For supported road journeys it attempts to retrieve a route; otherwise it shows a practical estimate.

Are flight distances exact?

Flight distance is based on the great-circle distance between the selected locations. Actual flight paths can vary.

Can I calculate fuel cost?

Yes. Enable the optional fuel calculator and enter fuel efficiency, price and currency.
