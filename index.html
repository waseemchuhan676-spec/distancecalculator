How to Calculate Distance Between Two Cities

Whether you are planning a road trip, calculating corporate mileage expenses, or checking flight distances, this distance calculator provides accurate measurements in both kilometers (km) and miles (mi).

1. Driving Distance vs Straight-Line Flight Distance

Straight-line distance (as the crow flies) uses the spherical haversine formula to compute the shortest geographical arc across Earth's surface. Driving distance tracks actual roads, expressways, and bridges via public road routing data, typically making the driving path 15% to 35% longer than air distance.

2. Travel Time Calculations

Travel times are calculated using typical cruising speed profiles for each mode (e.g. 80 km/h for driving, 800 km/h for flights). Actual duration varies depending on traffic conditions, road construction, weather, and rest stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate distance between two places?

Enter your starting location and destination, choose your travel mode (Car, Motorbike, Bus, Train, Flight, or Bicycle), and click 'Calculate Distance' to view total road distance, flight distance, estimated travel time, and optional fuel cost.

What is the difference between route distance and straight-line distance?

Straight-line distance (as the crow flies) is the shortest geographical path between two coordinates calculated using the haversine formula. Route distance is the actual driving or road distance following highways and roads, which is typically 15% to 35% longer than straight-line distance.

Can I calculate distance between cities in different countries?

Yes, the World Distance Calculator supports global locations including cities, towns, airports, and geographical landmarks worldwide.

How is flight distance calculated?

Flight distance is calculated using the Great-Circle Haversine formula based on Earth's spherical curvature, representing the direct aerial flight corridor between two airports or cities.

Can I calculate driving or road distance by car?

Yes. Select the Car / Driving mode to get road route distance along public highways powered by open road routing algorithms.

Can I estimate fuel cost for a road trip?

Yes. Check the 'Include trip fuel cost' box, select your efficiency unit (km/L, L/100km, or MPG), enter your vehicle efficiency and fuel price, and choose your currency to get an instant cost and consumption estimate.

Can I switch between kilometers and miles?

Yes. Toggle between the KM and Miles buttons in the results panel to view all distance measurements in your preferred unit.

Are travel times exact?

Travel times are based on typical speed averages (e.g. 80 km/h for driving, 800 km/h for flights). Actual duration varies based on traffic, weather, road conditions, and stops.
