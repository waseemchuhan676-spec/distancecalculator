About World Distance Calculator

World Distance Calculator is a free, web-based geographical measurement and journey planning tool designed to provide fast, reliable distance, flight corridors, and driving time calculations between any two locations globally.

Our Calculation Methodology

We combine two complementary measurement models to give users accurate geographical and travel data:

  • Great-Circle Spherical Distance: Using the Haversine trigonometric formula, we compute direct straight-line (as the crow flies) aerial distances based on Earth's mean radius of 6,371 kilometres.
  • Road & Highway Routing: Road distances and paths are computed using open routing data derived from the OpenStreetMap network via the Open Source Routing Machine (OSRM).

Transparency & Limitations

While our tool provides high-fidelity route and fuel estimates, real-world driving conditions depend on live road closures, weather, vehicle weight, and individual driving style. Flight distances reflect direct airspace corridors rather than commercial airline flight plans. Transit estimates reflect corridor approximations rather than scheduled train or bus timetables.

Open Data Attribution

Map tiles and geocoding data are provided by OpenStreetMap contributors. Routing is powered by OSRM.
