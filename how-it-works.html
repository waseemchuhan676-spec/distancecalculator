How Distance Is Calculated

A transparent breakdown of the geographic algorithms, geocoding pipelines, and routing engines powering World Distance Calculator.

1. Location Geocoding (Nominatim OpenStreetMap)

When you type a city name or address into the search input, our system queries the Nominatim OpenStreetMap Geocoder. This translates human-readable place names into precise WGS 84 geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude).

To preserve search accuracy and prevent coordinate mismatches, any manual modification to the city input immediately invalidates previous coordinates until a new verified location is selected.

2. Straight-Line Geodesic Distance (Haversine Formula)

Earth is an oblate spheroid with a mean radius of 6,371.0 kilometers (3,958.8 miles). To compute the true shortest path between two points across this curved surface, we execute the Haversine equation:

Δφ = lat₂ − lat₁ (in radians)
Δλ = lon₂ − lon₁ (in radians)
a = sin²(Δφ / 2) + cos(φ₁) ⋅ cos(φ₂) ⋅ sin²(Δλ / 2)
c = 2 ⋅ atan2( √a, √(1 − a) )
Distance (km) = 6371.0 × c

3. Geodesic Flight Arc (Spherical Linear Interpolation)

On flat 2D Web Mercator map projections, straight lines do not represent the true shortest path on a sphere. To accurately depict commercial flight corridors, we compute a Slerp (Spherical Linear Interpolation) arc consisting of 100 intermediate geodesic points along the Great Circle vector.

4. Driving Route Distance (OSRM Routing Engine)

Unlike straight-line flight paths, automobiles must navigate physical road networks. We query the Open Source Routing Machine (OSRM), which leverages high-performance Contraction Hierarchies over OpenStreetMap road graphs to determine:

  • Fastest legal driving route and total turn-by-turn distance.
  • Speed limit restrictions and highway hierarchies.
  • One-way streets, bridges, and tunnels.

5. Fuel Economy & Trip Expense Formulas

Our platform handles global fuel economy standards without approximation distortion:

  • km/L: Fuel Volume (L) = Distance (km) ÷ Efficiency (km/L)
  • L/100 km: Fuel Volume (L) = (Distance (km) ÷ 100) × (L/100km Rating)
  • MPG (US): Fuel Volume (Gal) = Distance (miles) ÷ MPG
  • Total Cost: Fuel Volume × Fuel Price per Unit × Trip Multiplier (1 for one-way, 2 for round-trip)
ℹ️ System Limitations: While OSRM routing reflects current road topology, it does not factor in real-time emergency road closures, active weather squalls, or live traffic delays. Drivers should always consult local highway alerts during extreme weather.
