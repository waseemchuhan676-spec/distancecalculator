Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries regarding distance formulas, road routing, fuel economy calculations, and website privacy.

Distance Calculations & Routing

What is the difference between straight-line distance and driving distance?

Straight-line distance is the direct geodesic distance across the Earth's surface ("as the crow flies") calculated via the Haversine formula. Driving distance follows vehicular highways and municipal roads, which are typically 15% to 35% longer due to natural terrain and road geometry.

Why do some cities show different distances on different websites?

Different mapping platforms use different reference anchor points (such as the historic city hall, geographical center, or central post office) and may use different routing algorithms (e.g. prioritizing the shortest distance vs. fastest highway time).

How does the flight arc work on the map?

We render a 100-point Spherical Linear Interpolation (Slerp) curve. On a flat 2D Mercator map, a true straight line across the spherical Earth appears as a curved arc, reflecting actual commercial airline flight corridors.

Fuel & Expense Estimation

How does the calculator handle different fuel economy units?

Our tools natively support km/L (Kilometers per Liter), L/100 km (Liters per 100 Kilometers), MPG US, and MPG UK (Imperial) with mathematically verified formulas.

Can I calculate round-trip expenses?

Yes. Simply select the "Round-trip (×2)" option on our calculators to automatically calculate total return distance, fuel consumption, and journey costs.

Privacy & Device Permissions

Does World Distance Calculator track my location?

No. When you click the "GPS" button, your browser prompts you for permission to access your device's coordinates. This data remains strictly client-side inside your browser and is never stored on our servers.

Is this service free to use?

Yes, World Distance Calculator is 100% free for all users worldwide. No account registration or credit card is ever required.
