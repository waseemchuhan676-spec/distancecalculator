1. General Information & Estimation Nature

All calculations, route distances, travel durations, driving directions, and fuel expense figures presented on this website are provided for general informational and travel planning purposes only. While we employ rigorous mathematical formulas (such as the Haversine equation) and industry-standard routing algorithms (such as Project OSRM and OpenStreetMap), results represent theoretical and historical computational estimates rather than real-time guarantees.

2. Road Conditions & Dynamic Factors

Actual driving distances and travel times can be influenced by variables outside our control, including but not limited to:

  • Temporary road closures, construction detours, and utility work.
  • Adverse weather conditions (such as snow, ice, heavy rainfall, or dense fog).
  • Traffic collisions, peak-hour bottlenecks, and emergency vehicle diversions.
  • Changes in municipal speed limits, turn restrictions, or one-way street directions.

3. Fuel Price & Currency Estimates

Fuel cost calculations are based on the user-entered fuel prices and vehicle economy numbers. Fuel prices at the pump fluctuate frequently across regions and service stations. Users should verify local fuel rates before undertaking journeys.
