Travel Overview: Multan to Lahore

Connecting the City of Saints (Multan) with the provincial hub of Lahore was historically a 6-to-7-hour drive along the congested N-5 highway through Khanewal, Mian Channu, Chichawatni, and Sahiwal. With the opening of the M-4 (Multan–Abdul Hakeem) and M-3 (Abdul Hakeem–Lahore) motorways, travel time is now cut in half to around 3.5 hours.

Motorway Details & Rest Areas

  • Motorway: Enter via Multan Shah Shams interchange to M-4, transition to M-3 at Abdul Hakeem interchange, and exit into Lahore near Faizpur / Ring Road.
  • Road Condition: Excellent 6-lane asphalt with electronic tolling (M-Tag).
  • Key Service Areas: Abdul Hakeem, Samundri, Nankana Sahib rest stops.
