Travel Overview: Lahore to Islamabad

The journey between Lahore (the cultural capital of Punjab) and Islamabad (the federal capital of Pakistan) is one of the most traveled corridors in South Asia. The primary route is via the 6-lane M-2 Motorway, which offers a smooth, controlled-access transit through the Salt Range.

Recommended Route: M-2 Motorway

The M-2 Motorway covers approximately 375 kilometers. Drivers typically exit Lahore through the Thokar Niaz Baig or Babu Sabu interchange and arrive at Islamabad through the Srinagar Highway (Kashmir Highway) interchange.

  • Speed Limit: 120 km/h (passenger cars) and 100 km/h (buses/heavy vehicles).
  • Popular Rest Areas: Sukheke, Sial, Bhera (famous for food and fuel), and Kalar Kahar.
  • Toll Tax: Electronic M-Tag is mandatory on the motorway.

Alternative Route: Grand Trunk Road (N-5)

The historical Grand Trunk Road (GT Road) passes through Gujranwala, Gujrat, Jhelum, and Rawalpindi. It is around 295 km but takes over 6 to 7 hours due to dense city traffic, signals, and intersections.
