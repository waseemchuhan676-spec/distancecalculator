Travel Overview: Dubai to Abu Dhabi

Traveling between the UAE's two largest emirates is an effortless drive along well-maintained multilane highways. Millions of residents and tourists commute between Dubai and Abu Dhabi every year for business, tourism, and flights.

Primary Highways

  • Sheikh Zayed Road (E11): The primary route connects Dubai directly to Abu Dhabi via Jebel Ali, Ghantoot, and Al Shahama.
  • Sheikh Mohammed Bin Rashid Al Maktoum Road (E311): A great alternative to bypass downtown Dubai congestion.
  • Speed Limits & Speed Buffer: In Abu Dhabi emirate, the posted speed limit is strictly enforced with zero buffer (usually 140 km/h). In Dubai, there is typically a 20 km/h grace buffer. Always monitor radar signs.
