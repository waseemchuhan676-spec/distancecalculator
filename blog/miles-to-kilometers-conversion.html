Miles to Kilometers: Formula & Worked Examples

Published on October 28, 2026 · 4 min read

Drivers visiting Canada, continental Europe, Australia, or Mexico often face road signs displaying speed limits and highway distances in kilometers instead of miles. Converting miles to kilometers correctly prevents speeding citations and ensures accurate navigation timing.

The Exact Conversion Formula

One international statute mile is legally defined as exactly 1.609344 kilometers:

Kilometers = Miles × 1.609344

For quick mental estimates, multiply your miles by 1.6, or add 60% to your mileage figure (for example, 50 miles + 30 = 80 kilometers).

Common Highway Speed Limit Conversions

When driving abroad, understanding speed limit equivalents is critical for safety:

  • 30 mph: 48.28 km/h (standard European urban limit: 50 km/h)
  • 55 mph: 88.51 km/h (standard single-carriageway limit: 90 km/h)
  • 65 mph: 104.61 km/h (standard dual-carriageway limit: 100–110 km/h)
  • 70 mph: 112.65 km/h (standard motorway limit: 110–120 km/h)
  • 80 mph: 128.75 km/h (high-speed interstate / French autoroute: 130 km/h)
